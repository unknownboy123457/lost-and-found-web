About Our Lost & Found Network

We provide a centralized, secure platform to catalog, safeguard, and return lost property effectively.

Every year, thousands of valuable personal items are misplaced in public venues, campuses, and transit stations. Our system offers an organized digital registry where finders can report discovered items and rightful owners can reclaim them safely.

To uphold the highest level of community trust, our team requires unique identifying details that only the true owner would know before authorizing an item handover at our official collection points.

Verified Claims

Every claim is inspected by administrators and verified against confidential owner details.

Official Collection Desks

Handovers happen exclusively at official, supervised venue checkpoints with photo ID verification.

Privacy Protected

Private identifying answers and claimant contact details are guarded and never shown publicly.
